03 / Manajemen risikoRisk management

Risiko dikenali sebelum mengganggu operasi.Risks identified before they disrupt operations.

Perseroan mengidentifikasi, menilai, memantau, dan memitigasi risiko yang dapat mengganggu kegiatan usaha. Pengendalian internal mencakup aspek keuangan, operasional, dan kepatuhan, melibatkan Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Unit Audit Internal, dan Komite Audit.The Company identifies, assesses, monitors, and mitigates risks that could disrupt its business. Internal control covers financial, operational, and compliance aspects, involving the Board of Commissioners, Directors, Internal Audit Unit, and Audit Committee.

04 / Whistleblowing

Laporkan pelanggaran dengan aman.Report a violation safely.

Karyawan, mitra, dan masyarakat dapat melaporkan dugaan pelanggaran kode etik, kecurangan, atau korupsi. Identitas pelapor dirahasiakan dan pelapor dengan iktikad baik dilindungi dari tindakan balasan.Employees, partners, and the public can report suspected breaches of the code of conduct, fraud, or corruption. Reporters' identities are kept confidential and good-faith reporters are protected from retaliation.

Email dan formulir khusus WBS:Dedicated WBS email and form: MenyusulTo follow

Alur penanganan yang diusulkanProposed handling flow

  1. Laporan diterimaReport received

    Pelapor mendapat nomor tiket untuk memantau status tanpa membuka identitas.The reporter receives a ticket number to follow progress without revealing their identity.

  2. Verifikasi awalInitial review

    Pengelola WBS menilai kecukupan bukti dan kewenangan penanganan.The WBS officer checks the evidence and who should handle the case.

  3. InvestigasiInvestigation

    Dilakukan oleh Unit Audit Internal, atau Komite Audit bila melibatkan Direksi.Handled by Internal Audit, or by the Audit Committee where Directors are involved.

  4. Tindak lanjutFollow-up

    Sanksi dan perbaikan dicatat, dan pelapor diberi tahu hasil akhirnya.Sanctions and remedies are recorded, and the reporter is told the outcome.
